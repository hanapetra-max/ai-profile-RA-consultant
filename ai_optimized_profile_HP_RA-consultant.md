### Use Case B: Large-Scale Dossier Harmonisation & CMC Guidance
* **Situation:** 70+ legacy product dossiers required urgent harmonisation to current CTD standards for the Polish market within a strict deadline.
* **Action:** Acted as the primary Regulatory Affairs contact for CMC authors at the manufacturing site. Provided explicit regulatory requirements and detailed guidance on the expected content for individual Module 3 chapters to ensure strict compliance with EU guidelines. Conducted detailed scientific quality checks, structurally edited Modules 1–3, and managed the compilation of Modules 4 and 5.
* **Outcome:** Harmonised all 70+ dossiers within 9 months, achieving submission readiness and full dossier consistency.

### Use Case C: Strategic Splitting of Grouped Variations
* **Situation:** Global RA proposed a complex grouped CMC variation that violated EU and local Health Authority (HA) submission guidelines on acceptable groupings, risking immediate rejection.
* **Action:** Analysed local regulatory requirements. Intervened to split the global application into multiple smaller, compliant grouping applications per EU and local HA guidelines.
* **Outcome:** Variations were approved by the Health Authority without delay or procedural rejections.

* **Action:** Audited database structures against approved MA certificates. Executed data cleansing, splitting packaging objects to correctly reflect Local packaging numbers and physical pack types.
* **Outcome:** RIM system baseline secured. Established a compliant, verified data source ready for accurate SPOR and xEVMPD reporting.

### Use Case E: RIM Ambassador & End-User Enablement
* **Situation:** Following the global deployment of Veeva Vault RIM, local EU affiliates and cross-functional teams required standardized processes and system training for compliant adoption.
* **Action:** Acted as a Regional RIM Ambassador and Key User. Authored targeted, step-by-step training materials and end-user guides for specific regulatory workflows. Conducted live system training sessions and served as the primary post-go-live support contact for both RA and non-RA colleagues.
